About Us

Designing Experiences,
Not Just Spaces

"Architecture is not about building — it's about the quality of life of the people who inhabit the spaces we create."

Vaibhavi and Vandan Architects (VAVA) is an Ahmedabad-based architecture and interior design studio that believes every space tells a story. With over a decade of experience, we bring a meticulous design approach that blends aesthetics with functionality.

From luxurious apartments and bespoke villas to serene landscapes and dynamic workspaces, our portfolio spans diverse typologies — each crafted with precision, passion, and a deep understanding of our clients' aspirations.
